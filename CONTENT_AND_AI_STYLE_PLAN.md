# Rasa Ecology Content and AI Style Plan

## Summary

Build enough Rasa Ecology writing material for AI tools to understand the brand's SEO blog voice, structure, and preferred explanations.

The goal is not model fine-tuning initially. The goal is a **style corpus + prompt pack** that can be used with ChatGPT, Claude, Codex, or future internal tools.

Chosen default:

- AI style work focuses first on Rasa Ecology's SEO blog voice, not personal founder content.

Estimated content/style foundation: **2-3 weeks alongside publishing**

## Phase A: Style Foundation — 2 days

Create a `Rasa Ecology Editorial Style Guide`.

Include:

- Brand voice: grounded, ecological, practical, clear
- Audience: farmers, gardeners, sustainability-focused buyers, ecology-curious readers
- Reading level: accessible but not shallow
- Tone: educational, not hype-heavy
- Avoid: generic AI phrasing, exaggerated claims, unsupported science
- Preferred structure: hook, context, practical explanation, ecological mechanism, product-adjacent takeaway
- SEO rules: one target keyword, natural headings, concise meta descriptions
- Citation rules: use credible sources for scientific claims

Deliverables:

- Style guide
- Example intro patterns
- Example conclusion patterns
- Do/don't phrase list
- SEO checklist

Acceptance criteria:

- Writers can use the guide before drafting
- AI prompts can reference the guide directly
- The guide gives concrete examples, not just abstract adjectives

## Phase B: Seed Content Corpus — 1-2 weeks

Write 10-15 high-quality seed posts before scaling output.

Suggested clusters:

- Soil ecology basics
- Compost and microbial life
- Biochar and soil structure
- Water retention and living soil
- Native plants and biodiversity
- Regenerative gardening
- Urban ecology
- Kitchen waste to soil health
- Mycorrhizae basics
- Why chemical-heavy soil practices fail over time

For each post, create:

- Final blog article
- Brief outline
- Target keyword
- Search intent
- Meta description
- Notes on why it sounds like Rasa Ecology

Acceptance criteria:

- At least 10 published-quality posts
- Each post follows the same editorial system
- AI can be prompted with the corpus to generate close matching drafts

## Phase C: AI Prompt Pack — 1 day

Create reusable prompts:

- Generate blog outline in Rasa style
- Rewrite draft in Rasa style
- Expand Notion notes into article
- Generate SEO title/meta options
- Generate image ideas
- Create social snippets from blog post
- Check article against editorial style guide

Acceptance criteria:

- Prompts reference the style guide and seed examples
- Outputs are consistent enough for human editing
- Edwin can use prompts without technical setup

## Phase D: Content Calendar — 0.5 day

Create a 12-week publishing calendar.

Default cadence:

- 2 posts/week for first 8 weeks
- 1 post/week afterward
- One cluster at a time to build topical authority

Acceptance criteria:

- Each planned post has keyword, title, search intent, and internal-link target
- Calendar prioritizes SEO clusters over random topics

## Timeline

| Task | Estimate |
| --- | ---: |
| Editorial style guide | 2 days |
| 10-15 seed posts | 1-2 weeks |
| AI prompt pack | 1 day |
| 12-week content calendar | 0.5 day |
| Content/style foundation total | 2-3 weeks |

## Working Method

Use this order for each article:

1. Pick topic from the content calendar
2. Define target keyword and search intent
3. Draft outline
4. Write article in Rasa Ecology voice
5. Check against style guide
6. Add metadata: title, description, tags, slug, hero image idea
7. Publish through Notion once ready

## Assumptions

- Content is optimized for long-term SEO, not short-term social virality.
- AI assists with outlines, rewrites, and consistency checks, but humans approve final posts.
- Scientific or ecological claims should be sourced from credible references.
- Early content should build topical authority around soil ecology, compost, biodiversity, and regenerative growing.
